## MSATA Brightness Ranges

Limiting bright (Saturation) and faint (S/N=20) magnitudes for target acquisition reference stars. All TA exposures are acquired with Ngroups = 3.

| Readout mode | S/N = 20 (F110W) | S/N = 20 (F140X) | S/N = 20 (CLEAR) | Saturation (F110W) | Saturation (F140X) | Saturation (CLEAR) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **NRSRAPID** | 22.0 | 23.0 | 23.8 | 19.5 | 20.6 | 21.3 |
| **NRSRAPIDD1** | — | — | 24.5 | — | — | 21.9 |
| **NRSRAPIDD2** | — | — | 24.9 | — | — | 22.2 |
| **NRSRAPIDD6** | 24.0 | 25.0 | 25.7 | 21.3 | 22.3 | 23.1 |

*Note: NRSRAPIDD6 replaced NRSRAPID (NRS) for MSATA for improved cosmic ray mitigation.*
