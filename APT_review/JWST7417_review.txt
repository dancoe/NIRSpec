JWST 7417
Brightest & Farthest: Confirming intrinsically luminous z~10-12 Galaxies in COSMOS
PI: Caitlin Casey

Observing Description:
This program consists of 16 NIRSpec MSA pointings focused on z>10
 luminous galaxy candidates in the COSMOS-Web field. 
 The MSA is configured to capture these candidates as well as 170
 additional galaxies in the field per pointing in PRISM mode to obtain
 R~200 spectroscopy for ~2700 galaxies.  We use the NRSIRS2RAPID
 readout pattern with 29 groups per integration and 1 integration per
 exposures x 3 dithers x 3 NIRCam filter switches.  We use a 3 shutter
 slitlet nod pattern with no dither to optimize time spent integrating
 our spectra without incurring overheads for reconfiguring the MSA.

In parallel, we obtain NIRCam imaging in F115W, F115W, F150W SW
filters and F356W, F410M, and F444W LW filters These filters will
enable a transient search at z>3 at the highest redshifts and
deepen imaging for non-transient science.

Meteroid Zone Justification:
25% of our pointings require a PA in the range of the December observability window for COSMOS, which is in the meteroid risk zone.  These PAs are ideal to maximize science output by doubling the number of high priority targets on a mask.  For this reason, the scientific payoff of using this PA vs the nominal non-risky PA is worth it.  Note that half of observations so far observed in COSMOS have been taken in the meteroid risk zone.
